Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services.

Do you only help new businesses?

No. COREBIZ supports both new entrepreneurs setting up their foundation and established businesses looking to streamline recurring statutory filings.

Can I use COREBIZ if I already have a CA?

Yes. COREBIZ regularly provides support for needs outside your existing arrangement—such as Trademark registrations, PF/ESIC handling, or documentation review.

Do you provide only one-time services?

We provide both one-time setup services (such as incorporation or trademark filings) and ongoing recurring retainers for monthly GST, PF/ESIC, and annual tax management.

Can you help me understand what registrations my business needs?

Yes. We can review your business model, projected turnover, and location to provide a customized roadmap of required statutory registrations.

Do you provide legal and property services?

COREBIZ coordinates property conveyancing and legal documentation within the applicable scope. Where a matter requires a designated legal specialist, we coordinate with the relevant credentialed counsel.

Can you handle PF & ESIC for my employees?

Yes, we offer ongoing PF/ESIC statutory support, registration, monthly statement filings, and records maintenance based on your company size.
